# LivestockOS
An Albanian-first, English-optional farm application for roughly 200 goats with support for additional species. The owner operates the farm from a computer and installed phone PWA. The veterinarian reads the same authorized farm without mutation rights.

Animals, immutable clinical history, vaccinations, tasks, calendar, backups and local photographs remain available offline. IndexedDB v5 and backup v1 are preserved. Secure device sessions synchronize validated records through the existing M8 protocol. Initial reconciliation remains explicit.

Accounts are adi (Admin) and vet (Veterinarian). No credentials belong in source. Clinical dates, doses and withdrawal periods come only from owner or veterinary input. Background notifications require explicit device consent; cached calendar reminders remain usable without push.

The approved dashboard mockup and attached final-release brief define the current design. Production data, credentials and deployments require their existing safety boundaries. No photo cloud storage, paid service or ERP expansion is part of this release.
